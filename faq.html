Questions, answered

Everything to know before you book.

How is pricing decided?

Flat-rate by home size, bedrooms, bathrooms, condition, service type and add-ons.

Do I need to be home?

No. Many clients arrange access in advance. We will confirm details when we schedule.

Do you bring supplies and equipment?

Yes. Pricing includes supplies, equipment and travel.

Do you offer recurring service?

Yes: weekly, bi-weekly, monthly or one-time.

How does the referral reward work?

A new customer and the person who referred them each get $25 off after the new customer completes their first paid cleaning. Rewards cannot be combined with other promotions or exchanged for cash.

What if my home is in poor condition?

No judgment. Tell us in the form and send photos so your quote is accurate.
